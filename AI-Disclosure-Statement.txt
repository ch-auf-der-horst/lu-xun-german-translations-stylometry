# AI Disclosure

## Summary
This project was conceived and carried out by Christoph auf der Horst, who designed the study, selected and prepared the corpus, checked and corrected the annotations in the original reference sample, reviewed all discrepant dependency attachments, decided which corrections to adopt and developed and revised the German working text of the manuscript. Generative AI (OpenAI ChatGPT/Codex) was used as an assistant for programming, annotation pre-processing, discrepancy review, quantitative recalculation, work on the manuscript and repository preparation. The review status of additional corrections, supplementary labels and the English manuscript translation is specified below.

## Author's contributions
- Research question and study design
- Corpus selection and preparation of source material
- Individual checking and correction of the original reference annotations
  (180 subordinate-clause cases, 45 dependency trees)
- Review of all 127 discrepant dependency attachments
- Decisions on which proposed corrections to adopt
- Inspection of the original source files, transcription of the affected passages and
  visual verification of text repairs against the source scans
- Interpretation of the results
- Development and revision of the German working text and confirmed revision passages
- Choice of licences for the public package

## Use of AI assistance
ChatGPT/Codex supported the author in:
- writing and revising analysis scripts
- pre-annotating the reference sample
- identifying candidate corpus and syntactic corrections
- reviewing dependency-attachment discrepancies
- recalculating lexical, readability, PCA and Delta analyses, comparing preprocessing
  variants and performing numerical cross-checks
- regenerating concordances and producing figures
- drafting and revising German manuscript passages for the author's review and approval,
  translating the approved passages into English and integrating them into the manuscript
- preparing the public code and results package, including licence files and metadata
